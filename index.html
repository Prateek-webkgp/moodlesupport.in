Operational Work

Systems I built from scratch

Real problems. Production solutions. Every system below is live and serving 10,000+ institutional users at IIT Patna.

01 / AUTOMATION

Process Automation — Class Communication System

Institutional communication ran on a 10–15 minute manual process: find the course, look up email groups, open Outlook, compose, send. No audit trail. No reliability. I rebuilt it as a single-trigger automation — one message, instant execution, automatic logging.

Before: 15 minutes manual · After: under 10 seconds · Serving: 10,000+ users, 60+ units
02 / REPORTING TOOL

Centralised Grade Reporting & Faculty Self-Service

Faculty spent 2–3 hours per week manually pulling grade data across multiple programs with no central view. Built a role-based reporting tool with multi-program visibility, cohort filters, and direct data access — accessible from a single interface.

Impact: 300+ faculty · Time saved: 2–3 hrs/week per person · Support tickets: near zero
03 / RECORDS MANAGEMENT

Academic Records Management — Transcript System

End-of-semester transcript generation required navigating dozens of screens per student — 15–20 minutes per record. Deployed a custom records system with cohort, batch, and programme filters, output formatted to institutional standards, exportable directly.

Before: 20 minutes/record · After: under 60 seconds · Zero manual exports
04 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Backup & Recovery Pipeline Rebuild

Inherited a manual backup process — triggered by hand each semester, no verification, storage bloat accumulating with no cleanup. A failed restore before exams locked out faculty and students. Rebuilt as fully automated pipeline: scheduled, verified, alerted, self-managing.

Backup time: 30% reduction · Restore failures: zero since rebuild · Storage: fully automated
05 / ONBOARDING

Zero-Disruption Semester Onboarding — 10,000+ Users

Every semester: thousands of new participants needing accounts, enrolments, cohort assignments, and role configurations — under tight pre-semester deadlines. Designed a repeatable, automated onboarding process with pre-validation, dry-run staging, and bulk provisioning. Result: 100% Day-1 access, every semester.

Scale: 10,000+ per semester · Day-1 access: 100% · Failures: zero
06 / IDENTITY & ACCESS

Enterprise SSO — Google & Microsoft Integration

10,000+ institutional users authenticating through separate systems — password resets, login failures, and support overhead consuming significant admin capacity. Configured OAuth2-based Single Sign-On across both Google Workspace and Microsoft, eliminating an entire category of support tickets.

Users: 10,000+ · Auth tickets: eliminated · Providers: Google + Microsoft
